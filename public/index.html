Platform Capabilities

Engineered for speed, safety, and reliability

Per-Form Rate Limiting

Rate limits are enforced per Form ID and client IP, isolating traffic and preventing bad actors from taking down other forms.

Formula Injection Defense

Automatic sanitization for cells starting with =, +, -, or @ to neutralize spreadsheet macro exploits.

Instant Email Alerts

Get notified the instant a visitor submits. Configurable per-form destination email with formatted inquiry summaries.

Live Submissions Log

Review all historical submissions inside your SaaS dashboard with live status indicators even without opening Google Sheets.

Open CORS Architecture

Submit from any domain, static site, JAMstack app, or client-side framework with zero CORS blockers.

Fail-Safe Fallback

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to verify an app with Google Cloud?

No! With our shared service account approach, all you do is share your Google Sheet with our service account email (as Editor). There is zero OAuth consent screen or app verification needed.

Can I create multiple forms for different websites?

Yes. You can create as many forms as you need. Each form has its own Form ID, custom fields, destination Google Sheet, and notification recipient.

What happens if Google Sheets goes down or has an error?

FormBridge never silently drops your leads. Submissions are saved to the internal database and appended to a fallback log, and email alerts are still dispatched.
